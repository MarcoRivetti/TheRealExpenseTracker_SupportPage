# The Real Expense Tracker — Support

The Real Expense Tracker turns your bills and your pay into one number: the transfer to make each payday so every bill is covered.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need an account?**
No. There's no sign-up and no login. Your data stays on your device.

**Does the app connect to my bank or move money?**
No. The app never connects to a bank and never moves money. It works out the transfer from what you enter, and you make the transfer yourself.

**How do I sync between my iPhone, iPad and Mac?**
Sync uses your private iCloud. Sign in to the same Apple Account on each device and make sure iCloud is turned on. Changes can take a minute to appear on your other devices.

**My data isn't showing up on another device.**
Check that both devices use the same Apple Account, that iCloud is on, and that they're connected to the internet. Then open the app on both devices and give it a minute.

**What is Expense Tracker Plus?**
An optional, one-time purchase with no subscription. It adds Home and Lock Screen widgets, What if, savings and debt one and three years ahead, more themes, celebration styles, and a Today screen arranged your way. The budget itself is fully usable without it.

**I bought Plus. How do I get it on another device?**
Open Settings (the gear button), tap Expense Tracker Plus, then tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with. Plus can also be shared with your family through Family Sharing.

**Can I get a refund?**
Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

**I'm not getting reminders.**
Make sure notifications are allowed for the app in the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad (Notifications), or in System Settings on your Mac.

**How do I start over?**
Use Reset all data in the app's Settings. If iCloud sync is on, this also clears your data on your other devices.

**How do I delete my data completely?**
Delete the app from your device. To remove synced data, go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage on iPhone or iPad, or System Settings → [your name] → iCloud on Mac, and delete the app's data there.
